## Admission gates and handoff boundary

Keep admissions closed until independent encryption/security review, staffed moderation and two-person recovery, accurate nonprofit/legal/contact disclosures, usable operational alerts and an agreed operating budget. The marketing reflects the intended nonprofit/public-good model; incorporation or tax-exempt status must not be invented. The repository remains private until the owner chooses to publish it.

Physical-device signing, APNs and universal-link association need real Apple credentials/device testing and are separate from the completed simulator/hosted encryption checks. Google/GitHub OAuth, Datadog and technical proof-of-humanity remain deferred under the agreed MVP scope.

Completion still requires the outstanding hosted checks, final later-change verification, cleanup of temporary resources and fixtures, and an operational handoff. Existing success records do not establish untested behavior or remove the human/security admission gates.
