## Admission gates and completion boundaries

Keep `SIGNUP_ENABLED=false` outside explicitly coordinated synthetic signup windows; those windows still require email-bound, expiring invitations. The initial three-account staging window is closed again, and production has remained closed. Do not admit real members before independent crypto/security review, staffed moderation and two-person recovery, accurate nonprofit/legal/contact disclosures, operational alerts and a verified restore drill.

The repository is private by owner instruction. Apache-2.0 licensing does not mean the source is publicly downloadable. PWA software still trusts operator-delivered code; end-to-end encryption does not protect against a malicious client update or recipients copying content. Staff receive necessary operational metadata and only report content explicitly disclosed by a member.

Live TLS, database bindings, protected health, delivered signup verification and the hosted social checks listed above are verified. Completion still requires full account deletion/recovery verification, web↔iOS interoperability, remaining installed-PWA checks, telemetry/performance alert delivery, hosted restore and capacity measurements, final verification of later fixes, and operational handoff. Passing local or CI tests alone does not satisfy those deployment requirements.
