# Porchday implementation and verification record

Updated October 25, 2026. Porchday is deployed at [porchday.com](https://porchday.com) and [staging.porchday.com](https://staging.porchday.com), with separate databases and private storage. Both applications keep `SIGNUP_ENABLED=false`. The [private repository](https://github.com/bobbyhwilder/porchday) is Apache-2.0 licensed. Hosted acceptance remains in progress; this record does not authorize real-member admission.

## Current deployment

The application release is commit `c72e15f`: staging version `b1879dad-65aa-4781-924e-7003691429d1`, production version `701a2c3e-aa7f-4f5a-8928-f00e4547d646`. Public and protected health returned `ok`, with no protected operational issues. The delivered HTML, JavaScript, CSS, service worker and four wallpaper assets were compared byte-for-byte with the exact release build in both environments.

The visual direction is a homemade personal page: burnt orange, mustard and cream, dense paper panels, hard borders, small badges, and a dark tiled background. A new empty profile receives one of four curated wallpaper IDs, encrypted and saved once. Existing profiles and concurrent edits win. Both clients provide a picker and a standard-theme view; no arbitrary CSS or external background URLs are accepted. Cosmetic initialization failure leaves encryption keys and account access intact. The [design document](../design/social-network-design.md) records the agreed product and privacy requirements.

Both databases now have 41 migrations through `040_restore_operator.sql`. The new operator grants are verified on staging and production without changing object ownership, RLS, or runtime-role isolation. The isolated restore retry is in progress; applying those grants is not evidence that a full restore passed.

## Admission gates and handoff boundary

Keep admissions closed until independent encryption/security review, staffed moderation and two-person recovery, accurate nonprofit/legal/contact disclosures, verified restore, usable operational alerts and an agreed operating budget. The marketing reflects the intended nonprofit/public-good model; incorporation or tax-exempt status must not be invented. The repository remains private until the owner chooses to publish it.

The native app builds and runs in the simulator without App Store enrollment. Physical-device signing, APNs and universal-link association need real Apple credentials/device testing and are separate from the completed simulator/hosted encryption checks. Google/GitHub OAuth, Datadog and technical proof-of-humanity remain deferred under the agreed MVP scope.

Completion still requires the outstanding hosted checks, final later-change verification, cleanup of temporary resources and fixtures, and an operational handoff. Existing success records do not establish untested behavior or remove the human/security admission gates.
