# Contributor code of conduct

This policy applies to Porchday issues, pull requests, reviews and project development spaces, including people representing the project there. It is separate from the social network's member rules and moderation process.

## Expected behavior

Treat people with respect. Discuss ideas and code on their merits, give actionable feedback, acknowledge mistakes, and accommodate different experience levels and accessibility needs. Disagreement is welcome; personal attacks and intimidation are not.

Do not harass, threaten, stalk or discriminate based on identity, background, disability, age, race, ethnicity, religion, nationality, gender, sexual orientation or other personal characteristics. Sexual harassment, unwanted sexual attention, demeaning language, impersonation and repeated unwanted contact are unacceptable. Respect requests to stop contact.

Protect privacy. Do not disclose another person's contact details, private conversations, credentials, recovery material or member content without authorization. Use synthetic examples in technical discussions. Do not retaliate against someone who reports a concern or participates in a review.

## Reporting

For abuse on GitHub, use the affected content's **Report content** control and select **Report abuse to GitHub Support** when offered. You can also report a user through **Block or Report → Report abuse**, or use [GitHub Support's abuse-reporting form](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse?category=report-abuse&report=other&report_type=unspecified). Follow [GitHub's reporting instructions](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) for the relevant content type and access requirements. Include relevant links and a brief factual description; do not open an ordinary issue containing sensitive details.

GitHub Support reviews these reports under GitHub's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service, including reports involving a repository owner. GitHub handles that process independently of Porchday's repository administration. It is not a general private Porchday conduct inbox and does not guarantee enforcement of every provision of this code.

Porchday currently has no separate private conduct inbox or independent internal appeals process. Concerns outside GitHub, and project conduct concerns that do not violate GitHub's rules, are not covered by this reporting route. Existing collaborators may use an established private project contact where appropriate, but this policy promises no additional contact, staffed response or deadline.

Report vulnerabilities through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Keep exploit details and private member data out of conduct reports.

## Review and enforcement

The repository owner, or a designated maintainer without a conflict of interest, may review evidence, ask for context, remove harmful material, require a change in behavior, or restrict participation and repository access. Responses should reflect severity, persistence and immediate risk. Serious threats or exposure of private information may require immediate restrictions.

Share report information only with people who need it to address the concern. Do not promise absolute secrecy or a response deadline the project cannot provide. Explain decisions and participation conditions where appropriate without exposing another person's private information.

Project reviewers should recuse themselves from matters involving their own conduct. GitHub handles its own enforcement decisions and review process; it does not provide an independent appeal of every Porchday repository decision.
