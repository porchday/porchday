# Porchday contributor code of conduct

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews and project development spaces. It covers contributors, maintainers and people representing the project in those spaces. It is separate from the social network's member rules and moderation procedures.

## Expected conduct

Treat people with respect. Discuss ideas and code on their merits, give actionable feedback, acknowledge mistakes, and accommodate different experience levels and accessibility needs. Disagreement is welcome; personal attacks and intimidation are not.

Do not harass, threaten, stalk or discriminate against people based on identity, background, disability, age, race, ethnicity, religion, nationality, gender, sexual orientation or other personal characteristics. Do not use sexual harassment, unwanted sexual attention, demeaning language, impersonation, or repeated unwanted contact. Respect a request to stop contact.

Protect privacy. Do not disclose another person's contact details, private conversations, credentials, recovery material, or member content without authorization. Use synthetic examples in technical discussions. Do not retaliate against someone who reports a concern or participates in a review.

## Reporting and current contact limitation

The repository is currently private. Collaborators who already have an established private project contact with the repository owner, [@bobbyhwilder](https://github.com/bobbyhwilder), should use that channel to request a confidential conduct review. Share only the information needed: what happened, relevant links or dates, and any immediate concern. Avoid posting sensitive details in an issue visible to all collaborators.

The profile link identifies the repository owner; it is not a private messaging endpoint. A dedicated conduct-reporting contact and an independent alternate for concerns involving the owner have **not been established in this repository**. Do not assume an unlisted email address exists. Before opening community participation or admitting real members, the owner must designate those contacts, confirm that they accept reports, and publish the usable instructions here. Until an independent alternate exists, the project cannot promise an independent review of reports involving the owner.

Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not include exploit details or private member data in an ordinary conduct report.

## Review and enforcement

The repository owner, or a designated maintainer without a conflict of interest, may review available evidence, ask the people involved for context, remove harmful material, require a change in behavior, or restrict participation and repository access. Responses should reflect the severity, persistence and immediate risk of the conduct. A serious threat or exposure of private information may require an immediate restriction.

Share report information only with people who need it to review or address the concern. Do not promise absolute secrecy or a response deadline that the project cannot provide. Explain the decision and any participation conditions to the affected people when doing so is appropriate and does not expose someone else's private information.

A person affected by a decision may request reconsideration through the same private contact, with relevant new facts or a conflict-of-interest concern. A reviewer should recuse themselves from a matter involving their own conduct; the independent-contact launch gate above must be resolved to make that review possible.
