## Ownership and launch gate

The repository does **not** yet contain a verified record identifying the legal owner of project names or marks, an assignment to a nonprofit entity, or a trademark registration. No registration or exclusive legal right is asserted here. The intended philanthropy-funded nonprofit operator must not be presented as legally established until that is confirmed.

Before public launch, the repository owner must identify the actual operating entity and any holder of branding rights, document any necessary assignments or permissions, verify the status of branding assets, and publish an accurate contact and permission policy. Domain control alone does not settle those questions. Until that work is complete, do not invent a rights holder, registration symbol or branding approval contact.

For now, existing collaborators may raise branding questions through their established private project contact with the repository owner. The linked GitHub profile is an identity reference, not a private reporting or permission endpoint.
